About me

Architecture with delivery in mind.

My platform experience includes Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Experience Cloud, and Salesforce CPQ, with hands-on background in Apex, Lightning, Flows, and REST integrations. I lead architecture reviews, shape technical designs, mentor teams, and stay engaged from planning through release.
